**Nothing happens when I double-click myco**
Check that you've fully extracted the ZIP file. Windows sometimes shows the contents of a ZIP like a folder, but you must actually extract it first. Right-click the ZIP, choose "Extract All," then run from the new folder.

Open your browser manually and type `http://localhost:8080` into the address bar. If that doesn't work, check the myco console window for alternate port numbers it may be using.

**I forgot my password**
Unfortunately, there's no recovery option — that's the price of top-tier encryption. Always store your password somewhere safe!
